Data compression is the process of modifying, encoding or converting the bits structure of data in such a way that it consumes less space on computer storage or transmission networks.
LZ77 is a dictionary-based lossless data compression algorithm published by Lempel and Ziv in 1977.
It achieves compression by replacing repeated occurrences of data with references to a single copy of that data existing earlier in the uncompressed data stream (the sliding window).
