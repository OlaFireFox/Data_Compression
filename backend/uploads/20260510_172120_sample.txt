Huffman coding is a lossless data compression algorithm. Each character in the source data is replaced by its Huffman code. Huffman coding works by assigning variable-length codes to input characters, with lengths of the assigned codes being based on the frequency of corresponding characters. The most frequent characters get the shortest codes and the least frequent characters get the longest codes. Huffman coding produces a prefix-free code that can be uniquely decoded.
